# Luxe Bear - Balloon Bar (LXB)

The dedicated balloon styling, luxury installation, and party decor showcase for Luxe Bear Studio.

## Ubiquitous Language & Glossary

**Occasion**:
The primary navigation and taxonomy entity. Customers search and browse by event milestone (Birthdays, Communions & Confirmations, Gender Reveals, Bridal Era, Gifts) rather than technical balloon structures.
*Avoid*: Category, Section, Tag.

**Installation**:
On-site assembled luxury balloon decor, including organic arches, half arches with backdrops, and easel welcome signs.
*Avoid*: Setup, Decoration, Rig.

**Organic Arch**:
An asymmetrical, flowing balloon arrangement created with varied balloon sizes (5" to 36") to produce a modern, organic cloud-like appearance.
*Avoid*: Standard balloon arch, Spiral arch.

**Dual-Layer Bubble Balloon**:
A specialty gender reveal or gift balloon featuring an inner opaque latex balloon encased within an outer transparent bubble. The inner balloon bursts to release colored confetti while the outer clear balloon remains intact.
*Avoid*: Pop balloon.

**Inquiry**:
A customer request for availability, date reservation, and custom pricing quote. Explicitly non-binding until confirmed by the studio owner.
*Avoid*: Order, Booking, Checkout.

**The Store Line Rule (Balloon Care Policy)**:
The liability boundary stating that once balloons are installed or physically handed over to the client, responsibility for temperature care, sharp hazard avoidance, and bursting transfers entirely to the client.
